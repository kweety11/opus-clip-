FAQ

Questions, answered

What can I do with the downloads?

Stills, GIFs and MP4 clips are licensed for internal reference use — treatments, mood boards, animatics and pitch decks. They are not for publication or resale.

How does team billing work?

A team starts at two seats for $16/month. When you add more members, you only pay for the time they actually use — pause a seat any time.

What makes the search “AI”?

Every shot is indexed with visual metadata — shot size, angle, movement, time of day, palette and content tags. Your queries, pasted images and Smart Feed interactions are matched against that index.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month and downgrade to Free at the end of the billing period.
